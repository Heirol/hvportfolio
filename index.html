About me

I spend most days on the part of a product people touch first: the type, the spacing, the moment a button answers back.

Ten years in, I still prefer small teams and long projects. Before going independent I led design at a mapping startup and taught interaction design two evenings a week. I write the front end myself, so what gets designed is what gets shipped.
