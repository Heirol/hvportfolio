# BRIEF: portfolio (dummy)

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The user asked for "a portfolio
homepage with 3 sections: About me, My works (a list of 3 items), Contact. Just use
dummy content." Everything below beyond that sentence is an authored decision, not
an interview answer. The person, projects and email are invented placeholders.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe:** confident, typographic, quiet between the loud parts. References: a Swiss
   exhibition poster, a type foundry specimen sheet, the closing card of a film.
2. **Journey (user's sequence):** About me, My works (3 items), Contact. Authored: a name
   hero opens it, because a portfolio's first fact is whose it is.
3. **Energy curve:** loud open (the name), quiet About, loudest Works, calm close.
4. **Feeling, stage by stage, and the one moment:** see the feeling curve below.
5. **The thing no other site does:** the project names physically gain weight as you
   scroll through them, and stretch under your pointer.
6. **Range:** editorial, toward typographic poster. Not premium-minimal grey.
7. **One world or distinct scenes:** distinct scenes. No world, no media.
8. **Assets:** none. No photos, no API key, no ffmpeg. Type is the imagery.

## Evidence vs assumption

- Evidence: the three sections, the 3-item works list, dummy content.
- Assumption: the person is a designer/developer named "Mara Lindqvist" (placeholder).

## Grammar

Typographic poster. Chosen because there are no assets and generated imagery would
produce plausible, forgettable frames. Filmic one-shot, continuous world and split
stage all need media. Gallery needs images of work. Live surface needs a real product.
Chaptered editorial and rhythmic cutlist need more acts than three sections earn.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | Caused by |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Arrival, confidence | The name fills the viewport edge to edge, then shrinks away under scroll |
| About me | Intimacy | Silence after the loud name: one short paragraph at reading size, lots of air |
| My works (PEAK) | Delight, curiosity | Each project name swells from hairline to black weight under the wheel, and stretches with the pointer |
| Contact | Calm resolution | The page inverts to paper, the smallest type on the site, a plain underlined email |

## The peak

"The project names get heavier as you scroll, and stretch when you move your mouse."
Lives in My works. Largest span on the page (3.6 viewport-heights). The About act
before it is the quietest act.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where the names of the work put on weight while you read them.

## Authored silence

The space above and below the About paragraph is deliberate air, not dead scroll.

## Score

| Act | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | `pin` + `kinetic` (chars) + scale from `--sc-p` | The name is the image; it assembles, then recedes |
| About me | `flow` + `in` | The only act that reads like a document, which is the point |
| My works | `pin` + `cue` + bespoke variable-axis signature | The frame holds, three names arrive in turn and gain weight |
| Contact | `flow` + `drift` + `magnet` | Ground inverts, the page stops moving and responds |

## Signature move

Variable-font mass: each project title is set in Anybody (wght 100 to 900, wdth 50 to
150). Its local scroll progress drives `wght` from hairline to black; pointer X drives
`wdth`. Coded in the page, the engine is untouched. Reduced motion: fixed 800 weight.
